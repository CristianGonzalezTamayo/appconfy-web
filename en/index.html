What is Confy

Confy is an app that helps businesses in Colombia verify bank transfer receipts. It compares the amount, date and time on each receipt with the notification Bancolombia sends to your email and shows you whether it matches.

For businesses that take Bancolombia transfers

Never get fooled by a fake receipt again.

Confy checks every receipt against Bancolombia’s real notification and shows you whether it matches.

The problem

What happens at the counter, solved.

Did the money actually arrive?

→Confy checks whether the bank’s notification reached your inbox and shows the result on the receipt.

Edited receipts that look real.

→Confy doesn’t judge whether the image looks real: it compares its data with the bank’s notification.

Opening the bank app every time someone pays.

→The status shows up in Confy. No need to log into your bank for every payment.

Arguing with an employee about a payment.

→Each receipt records who uploaded it and its status.

How it works

Three steps.

  1. 01

    Your employee uploads the photo

    They take a photo of the receipt the customer shows them and upload it to Confy.

  2. 02

    Confy checks it with the bank

    It finds the Bancolombia notification in your inbox and compares the payment details.

  3. 03

    You see “Verified”

    If the details match, the receipt shows as Verified in your app.

What Confy doesn’t do

It does one thing. No detours.

  • No opening the bank app.
  • No calling to ask if it arrived.
  • No trusting a screenshot.
  • No asking for your bank password.

What happens to your data

  • You connect Gmail or Outlook by signing in with Google or Microsoft.
  • Receipt photos are deleted after 30 days.
  • Payment details (amount, date, time, bank, number and status) are kept while the account exists and deleted when it’s removed.
  • If the owner deletes their account, everything is deleted. If an employee deletes theirs, their receipts stay with the business without their name.

FAQ

What people ask us most.

Which banks does it work with?

For now, with payments and transfers you receive in a Bancolombia account.

What email do I need?

Gmail or Outlook. You connect by signing in with your Google or Microsoft account. It must be the email where you receive Bancolombia’s notifications.

Does Confy ask for my bank password?

No. Confy doesn’t ask for your bank password.

What happens to receipt photos?

They’re deleted after 30 days. Payment details are kept while the account exists and deleted when it’s removed.

How do I delete my account?

The Delete account page explains how. If you’re the owner, everything is deleted; if you’re an employee, your receipts stay with the business without your name.

Does it work on iPhone?

For now, Confy is Android only.
