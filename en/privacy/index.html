Confy is an app that helps businesses in Colombia verify bank transfer receipts. This policy explains what data we use and why.

Data we collect

  • Account: email, name and role (owner or employee).
  • Receipts: the photo and details of each transfer (amount, date and time, bank).
  • Bank notification emails: if the owner connects a Gmail or Outlook account, Confy reads only emails from the bank to verify receipts.
  • SMS (Android only): if the owner enables it, Confy reads only messages from trusted bank senders to verify receipts. We ask for permission before enabling it. Other messages are checked only on the phone to identify the bank's; we do not store them or send them to our servers. If a message claims to be from Bancolombia but comes from a number that is not the bank's, we store only that number (and how many times it arrived) and an encrypted fingerprint of the message to detect fraud; never the message text.
  • Notification token: a device identifier used to send you alerts.

How we use it

Only to verify receipts, show their status and send you alerts. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties, except the providers that run the app: Supabase (database and storage), Google Firebase (notifications) and Google or Microsoft (email connection).

Retention and deletion

Receipt photos are automatically deleted after 30 days. Payment details (amount, date, time, bank, receipt number and status) are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete it. You can request deletion of your account and data by writing to soporte@appconfy.com. You can revoke access to your email or SMS at any time from your Google or Microsoft account and from your phone settings.

How to delete your account

  • In the app: open Confy, tap the ⋮ menu and choose Eliminar mi cuenta (Delete my account). Type ELIMINAR to confirm.
  • By email: write from your account's email address to soporte@appconfy.com with the subject "Eliminar mi cuenta". We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted: your account, your name and your email. If you are a business owner, everything is deleted: the business, all its receipts and photos, connected email accounts and invitations. If you are an employee, the receipts you sent remain in the business history for the owner, without your name.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is restricted per account.

Your rights

Under Colombian Law 1581 of 2012, you may access, update, correct and request deletion of your data by writing to soporte@appconfy.com.

Children

Confy is not directed to children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with its date.
